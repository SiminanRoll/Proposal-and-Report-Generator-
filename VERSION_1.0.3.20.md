# Version 1.0.3.20

## Client terminology controls

- Added a **Refer to this organization as** field to new workspaces.
- The field defaults to **practice** for dental clients.
- Suggested alternatives include **firm**, **hospital**, **business**, and **organization**.
- The value remains editable from the workspace under **Client wording**.
- Custom terms are accepted and normalized for safe client-facing use.

## Adaptive report and proposal language

The selected term now flows through:

- Live client presentations
- Technology Health Review PDFs
- Pre-meeting overviews and email drafts
- HIPAA questions and explanations
- Server and workstation planning language
- Prospect proposals, pricing labels, and authorization copy
- Application, workflow, support, and lifecycle recommendations

Existing saved workspaces that do not contain this field automatically default to **practice**.
