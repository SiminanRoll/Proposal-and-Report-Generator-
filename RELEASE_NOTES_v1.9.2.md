# Client Compass v1.9.2

## HIPAA recap polish

- Client-facing HIPAA recap views no longer show a `Not sure` / `Not sure / unanswered` metric when the count is zero.
- Remaining HIPAA metric cards automatically rebalance across the available space in the live presentation, screen report, and finished PDF.
- Non-zero Not sure/unanswered counts remain visible and continue to drive follow-up messaging.
