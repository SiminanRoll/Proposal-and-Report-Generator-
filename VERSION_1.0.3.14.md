# Version 1.0.3.14

## Conditional pre-meeting documents

- When the HIPAA review is turned off, the pre-meeting overview and drafted email contain no HIPAA questionnaire references.
- When the HIPAA review is turned on, the pre-meeting download becomes a packet containing:
  - The Technology Review overview
  - All 12 technology-related HIPAA readiness questions
  - Fillable response and notes fields
  - Clear language that the client may complete the questions now or wait and answer them during the meeting
- The drafted pre-meeting email automatically matches the selected workflow.
- Scores, findings, pricing, replacement recommendations, and other conclusions remain excluded from the pre-meeting document.
