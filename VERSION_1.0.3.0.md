# Proposal & Report Generator v1.0.3.0

## Onsite planning commitment scheduler

- The Recommended Next Step panel on server and larger replacement projects is now clickable during the client presentation.
- A polished calendar allows the presenter to select an onsite date, appointment time, and the assigned Technology Consultant while the client is on the phone.
- Common appointment times are available as quick choices, with a custom-time field for other availability.
- Confirming the appointment saves it to the local project record and immediately updates the Planning slide.
- A large green checkmark stamp appears with the message **Onsite Planning Scheduled** to mark the commitment moment.
- The scheduled date, time, and Technology Consultant are repeated on the final recap and included in both downloadable HTML and print-ready PDF output.
- Existing reports without an appointment remain fully compatible.
