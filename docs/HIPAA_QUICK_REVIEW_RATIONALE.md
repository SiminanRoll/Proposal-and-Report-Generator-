# HIPAA Quick Review Rationale

Reviewed against current HHS Office for Civil Rights material on August 3, 2026.

## Purpose

The in-app questionnaire is a technology-review readiness screen. It is intentionally shorter than a formal Security Rule risk analysis and should not be represented as an audit, certification, legal opinion, or guarantee of compliance.

## Current regulatory baseline

HHS describes the Security Rule currently in effect as requiring reasonable and appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for electronic protected health information. The rule is scalable and technology-neutral, so the exact safeguards and assessment method depend on the organization’s size, capabilities, infrastructure, costs, and risks.

HHS also describes risk analysis as foundational. A formal risk analysis must address the complete scope of electronic protected health information, threats and vulnerabilities, current security measures, likelihood, impact, risk levels, documentation, and ongoing updates. HHS does not prescribe one universal risk-analysis format.

The January 2025 cybersecurity changes remain a proposed rule on HHS’s Security Rule history and regulatory-initiatives pages as of this review. The quick questionnaire therefore maps to the rule currently in effect while still asking practical questions about modern controls such as multifactor authentication, endpoint monitoring, secure remote access, and recovery testing.

## Why 12 questions

The earlier detailed worksheet repeated related implementation specifications and created unnecessary data-entry work during a client technology review. The condensed set groups related topics while preserving the major readiness areas:

1. Security responsibility and written policies
2. Risk analysis and risk management
3. Workforce training and accountability
4. Access authorization and offboarding
5. Business associates and vendor oversight
6. Security incident response
7. Contingency planning, backup, and recovery
8. Facilities, workstations, devices, and disposal
9. Accounts, authentication, remote access, and secure transmission
10. Ongoing HIPAA review, qualified guidance, and change management
11. Endpoint protection, monitoring, and activity review
12. Backup monitoring and recovery verification

Questions 11 and 12 are the only technical-prefill items because imported managed-security and backup evidence can support them directly. A prefill remains a proposed answer until reviewed.

## Qualified guidance message

Question 10 asks whether the practice regularly reviews policies, staff training, and compliance needs with a qualified HIPAA consultant or compliance professional. The presentation and report explain that Client Compass is a technology readiness review that can identify possible weaknesses but does not determine HIPAA compliance. When ongoing qualified guidance is missing—especially alongside several No or Not sure responses—the client is encouraged to work with a qualified HIPAA consultant or compliance professional.

## Completion rule

A selected response completes a readiness question. Documentation is not required inside this app for every question. Optional notes and follow-up actions remain available when they improve the client conversation.

This usability choice does not remove the regulated entity’s separate obligation to maintain documentation required by the Security Rule or to document its formal risk analysis and risk-management decisions.
