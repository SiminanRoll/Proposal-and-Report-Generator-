# Phase 4 Acceptance — HIPAA Security Readiness

## Workspace and package language

The universal work area is a **Workspace**. The finished output is a **Package**. This language applies whether the organization is a current client, potential client, or legacy proposal recipient.

## Preparation-to-presentation rule

Every HIPAA question that is not fully completed during consultant preparation automatically appears in the live interactive presentation. Completed questions do not need to be asked again.

## Live review

The live review supports:

- Yes
- Partially
- No
- Not Applicable
- Skip for now
- Skip all remaining for now

A skipped question remains **Not Yet Assessed**. It is never converted to Not Applicable and remains available for a later follow-up session.

## Readiness reporting

The results distinguish:

- Confirmed-answer readiness
- Assessment completion
- Completion-adjusted displayed readiness

Not Yet Assessed questions reduce the displayed readiness score and trigger an **Incomplete Assessment** result. The final package identifies the skipped count and affected controls.

## Confirmation and history

The organization reviews the completed or explicitly deferred assessment, acknowledges responsibility for organization-provided information, and saves a dated snapshot. Snapshots support future comparison without overwriting the prior assessment state.

## Guardrails

The assessment is not a certification, formal audit, legal opinion, or guarantee of HIPAA compliance. The required disclaimer appears throughout the workflow and in final outputs.
