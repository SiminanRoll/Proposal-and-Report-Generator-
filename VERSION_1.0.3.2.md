# Version 1.0.3.2

## Unified HIPAA presentation

- Combines the former **HIPAA review** and **HIPAA readiness** navigation pages into one client-facing **HIPAA review** section.
- Adds an internal Questions / Review switch so the presenter can move between unanswered questions and the readiness result without adding another item to the presentation header.
- Automatically switches to the Review view when the remaining questions are answered or skipped.
- Preserves the ability to revisit a skipped question from the review result.

## Presentation header

- Rebalances the brand, navigation, and action-button columns.
- Allows navigation labels to shrink safely without moving beneath Download PDF or Close.
- Adds a compact desktop treatment and horizontal-scroll fallback for narrower screens.
- Applies to both client technology reports and potential-client proposals.
