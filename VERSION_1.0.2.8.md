# Proposal & Report Generator v1.0.2.8

## Client-facing Advantage 360 proposal

The potential-client proposal now speaks directly to the practice instead of describing the proposal product or report-building process.

### Introduction

- Uses **Prepared for [Practice Name]** as the eyebrow.
- Uses **Advantage 360** as the only cover title.
- Replaces the generator-style summary with a short explanation of what was reviewed, what the proposal recommends, the investment, and the next steps.
- Renames the cover metrics to:
  - **Needs attention now**
  - **Plan for**
  - **In good shape**

### What the client can expect

- Reframes the former “Why Advantage” slide around the client experience.
- Covers dental-specific support, active security, dependable backup and recovery, and planning before technology becomes urgent.
- Closes with **You should always know who to call.**

### What we found

- Uses the plain-language subtitle **The most important items to address now and plan for next.**
- Adds or replaces the lifecycle finding with a clear hardware-replacement statement based on the actual server, Cloud Plus backup server, and computer counts.
- Explains why replacement is recommended without exposing technical device names.

### Recommended plan

- Uses the direct title **A clear path forward.**
- Explains four client-facing steps: confirm details, replace and prepare, onboard and protect, and support and plan ahead.
- Replaces internal recommendation copy with four practical outcomes for the practice.
- Retains the included one-time project item count.

### Investment

- Uses **Your technology investment** and separates:
  - **Estimated one-time investment**
  - **Ongoing monthly support**
- Replaces internal SKU names with plain client-facing service and project names.
- Removes detected application-name dumps from the proposal presentation and export.
- Shows the agreed pricing-confirmation statement when required amounts are incomplete.
- Prevents authorization until every included required project item has a price.

### Export consistency

- The downloaded HTML/PDF-ready proposal uses the same client-facing wording, hardware replacement statement, line-item labels, plan, investment language, and authorization totals as the live presentation.
