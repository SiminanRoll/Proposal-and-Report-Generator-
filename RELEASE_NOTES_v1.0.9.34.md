# Client Compass v1.0.9.34

Map compass and workspace polish.

- Adds a navy-glass Client Compass hub to the donut center. Its needle smoothly points to the largest grouped donut section for the currently selected map metric and updates as map/segment selections change.
- Keeps the compass present in the no-data service-area shell with a quiet idle movement.
- Removes boxed hero containers from top-level page titles so Settings, Data Tools, Segment Manager, segment detail, Map, and Report Generator share the same background-stamped title treatment.
- Refines the saved-segment drawer into softer translucent blue glass with rounded edges and a thin themed scrollbar.
- Makes the segment drawer auto-dismiss on mouse leave and click-away while preserving drag/drop behavior.
- Expands and stabilizes the bottom map rail so selected state pills and Clear map filters stay inside the panel border.
