# Version 1.0.3.8

## Pre-meeting preparation

- Added a one-page, US Letter portrait **Technology Review — What to Expect** PDF.
- The overview prepares the practice for equipment, security, backup, planning, and HIPAA topics without exposing scores, findings, replacement recommendations, or pricing before the meeting.
- Added a **Draft pre-meeting email** button that opens the user's email application with the primary client contact, subject, and approved preparation copy filled in.
- The email reminds the client that no advance research is required and asks that someone familiar with HIPAA policies, onboarding, or security procedures attend when possible.
- Added a workspace reminder to attach the downloaded overview PDF to the drafted email.

## Existing workflow retained

- The interactive presentation remains the live-meeting experience.
- The final client PDF remains the post-meeting report and includes only outstanding HIPAA questions when follow-up is needed.
